How it works

  1. Download Remira and connect your CGM. Your 21-day free trial starts when your CGM first syncs. No card is needed, and one free trial is available per person.
  2. Subscribe in the app before day 21. Choose $8.99 a month or $59.99 a year. You pay with your Apple Account, and the subscription renews automatically until you cancel. If you do not subscribe, access ends when the trial does.
  3. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings. Go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Deleting your Remira account does not cancel your subscription.
When access ends, Remira alerts stop. If your trial or subscription ends, the app and all Remira alerts stop, including urgent-low alerts. Your Dexcom app's own alerts keep working. Remira is not a replacement for your CGM's alerts, so always keep them on.

Prices are in US dollars. The App Store shows the price in your local currency before you confirm. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. See our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy and Support page.
