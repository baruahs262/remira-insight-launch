Refund Policy

Cancelling, refunds, and your free trial — in plain language.

  • Your first 21 days are free. Cancel in that window and you are never charged.
  • Cancel any time, in a few taps. No phone call, no chat, no retention gauntlet.
  • Cancelling stops the next charge. You keep Premium until the period you already paid for ends.
  • We do not refund partial periods as a rule — but if something went wrong, write to us.

The details

Free trial

New family accounts receive a 21-day free trial with full access. One trial per family. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are never charged. We may modify or discontinue trials for new users at any time.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from your account page on remira-ai.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep access until then.

We will not make you call, email, or chat with anyone to cancel.

Refunds

We do not offer refunds for partial periods, except where required by law. If something has gone wrong, email support@remira-ai.com — we would rather sort it out than have you feel cheated.

How billing works

Subscriptions are purchased on remira-ai.com and processed by Paddle, our merchant of record. They are not purchased through the App Store, and Apple does not process, service, or refund them.

Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period until you cancel. You are charged the then-current price at the start of each period using the payment method on file. If your trial converts, the first charge occurs when the trial ends.

If a CGM service stops working

Remira is not affiliated with Dexcom, Abbott, or any CGM manufacturer, and we rely on the sharing services those companies make available to followers. If one of them changes, restricts, or discontinues that service, the affected feature will stop working — through no fault of ours, and with no entitlement to a refund beyond what our Terms otherwise provide.

If your subscription lapses

Your data is not deleted. You lose access to Remira's features until you resubscribe.

Questions

Write to support@remira-ai.com. We read every message, and most get a reply within one business day.
